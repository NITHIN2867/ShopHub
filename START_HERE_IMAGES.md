## 🎨 Free Image Sources

- **Unsplash** - unsplash.com (best overall)
- **Pexels** - pexels.com (reliable)
- **Pixabay** - pixabay.com (large library)
- **Freepik** - freepik.com (diverse content)
- **Burst** - burst.shopify.com (e-commerce focused)

---

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Search by product name** on Unsplash/Pexels
2. **Download largest size available**
3. **Compress using TinyPNG** (save bandwidth)
4. **Use exact filenames** from the guides
5. **Test in browser** after uploading

## ✨ Image Quality Tips

1. **Resolution:** Use highest resolution available
2. **Format:** Save as JPG for smaller file sizes
3. **Background:** Use white or light background for products
4. **Quality:** Ensure images are clear and well-lit
5. **Compression:** Compress to 100-200KB max

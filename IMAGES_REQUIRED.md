## 🔗 WHERE TO GET FREE IMAGES

### High Quality Stock Photo Sites:
1. **Unsplash** (unsplash.com) - Free, high-quality, no attribution needed
   - Search: "wireless headphones", "smartphone", "coffee maker", etc.

2. **Pexels** (pexels.com) - Free stock photos, very reliable
   - Best for product and lifestyle shots

3. **Pixabay** (pixabay.com) - Free images and videos
   - Great for furniture and home decor

4. **Freepik** (freepik.com) - Premium and free options
   - Excellent for electronics and tech

5. **Shopify Burst** (burst.shopify.com) - Free for commercial use
   - Great for e-commerce product images

6. **Picsearch** (picsearch.com) - Quick product searches

## 💾 HOW TO SAVE IMAGES

### Step 1: Create Directories
```bash
cd frontend/public/images
mkdir carousel electronics fashion home-appliances home-decor home-furniture
```

### Step 2: Save Images with Correct Names
- Download images and save with exact filenames as listed
- Use JPG format for smaller file sizes
- Optimize images (compress to ~100-300KB per image)

### Step 3: Compress Images (Optional but Recommended)
Use online tools:
- TinyPNG (tinypng.com)
- ImageOptim (imageoptim.com)
- Compressor.io

---

## 🎨 IMAGE OPTIMIZATION TIPS

1. **Resize to correct dimensions** before uploading
2. **Compress files** - Aim for < 200KB per image
3. **Use JPG for photos**, PNG for graphics
4. **Maintain aspect ratio** for consistent layouts
5. **Add alt text** in your database for accessibility
